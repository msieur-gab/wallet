🔍 HC1 Credential Verifier

Verify HC1-encoded credentials from wallet QR codes

💡 The data should start with "HC1:" and contain Base45-encoded credential information

How to verify credentials:
1. Open the Identity Wallet app
2. Expand a credential card
3. Use your phone camera to scan the QR code
4. Copy the scanned text (starts with "HC1:")
5. Paste it above and click "Verify Credential"
